What I can do for you

  • End-to-end autonomy stacks

    Take a mobile robot from bare hardware to navigating real environments on its own — mapping, localization, path planning and obstacle avoidance built on ROS2 and Nav2.

  • Perception & computer vision

    Give a robot eyes it can trust: LiDAR SLAM, camera-based object detection and tracking, and sensor fusion for reliable awareness of its surroundings.

  • Robot behavior & decision-making

    Design the logic that makes a robot act sensibly in the field with Behavior Trees — reactive, testable and mission-driven control across large systems.

  • Sensor & hardware integration

    Bring up and calibrate the physical layer — LiDAR, cameras, IMUs and microcontrollers wired over I2C, SPI and UART — so the software has clean data to work with.

  • Embedded & low-level firmware

    Write efficient C/C++ down to the microcontroller for tight, real-time control on constrained hardware where every cycle and byte counts.

  • Field deployment & debugging

    Get robots working where it actually matters — on-site testing, debugging live deployments and hardening systems until they're robust enough to rely on.
